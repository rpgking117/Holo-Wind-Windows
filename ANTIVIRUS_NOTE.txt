ANTIVIRUS NOTE
==============

Your AV is going to flag this. That's expected, here's why.

The mod hooks directly into Fallout 4's renderer at runtime, uses a proxy DLL
that sits in front of the real SDL2, and the setup exe drops a PowerShell
script to your temp folder and runs it. All three of those things are also
things malware does. The AV isn't wrong to be suspicious, it just doesn't know
what it's looking at.

It costs money to have the binaries code signed and I am broke :/

There's no actual malware here. The mod has to do all of this to make Morrowind
show up inside a Pip-Boy -- there's no clean way to do it. Add the mod folder,
your Fallout 4 folder, and your OpenMW folder to your AV exclusions before
installing and you'll be fine.

If you're not comfortable doing that, I get it, but the mod won't work without
it. You are welcome to run these files through an Ai to double check what they do.
